# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Luxury gift buyers and heritage-conscious shoppers across the Gulf, Egypt, Europe, and Africa — individuals seeking authentic, traceable dates and Madinah-sourced heritage goods (herbs, honey, prayer items, fragrances) for personal indulgence or considered gifting. Secondary audience: B2B buyers — hotels, corporate gifting programs, distributors, and institutional/diplomatic gifting — who reach out via the Wholesale inquiry page rather than a self-serve portal (store is on Shopify Basic, not Plus; no native B2B catalog).

Context: browsing on a phone or laptop, often researching a meaningful gift (Ramadan, Eid, corporate, personal) rather than doing routine restocking. They're comparing "considered luxury" against generic dates/gift retailers and against louder Gulf-luxury aesthetics — IRTH's job is to read as more restrained and more authentic than both.

## Product Purpose

IRTH (إرث, "heritage/legacy") sells luxury dates and heritage gifts sourced from Madinah Al Munawwarah — positioned as a bridge between authentic Islamic/Arabian heritage and contemporary luxury commerce. Success looks like: a visitor feels the atmosphere and provenance before they feel sold to, trusts the sourcing/Halal claims, and converts either as a retail purchase or a wholesale inquiry.

## Brand Personality

CALM · REFINED · RESPECTFUL · INSPIRING · TIMELESS. Core manifesto line: "Some things are not made… they are inherited." Positioning line: "Atmosphere Before Commerce." Voice is mystique-first and visual-led — minimal text, maximum meaning; colors whisper, not shout; photography and provenance detail carry the story more than copy does. Never loud, never trend-chasing, never over-styled.

## Anti-references

- **Generic AI-cream SaaS**: warm-beige/cream body backgrounds, tiny uppercase tracked eyebrows above every section, gradient text, identical card grids — the saturated 2026 AI-slop defaults.
- **Loud Gulf-luxury gold overload**: heavy gold gradients, ornate borders on every surface, maximalist ornamentation — the opposite of the brand's restraint. Gold (#B0885E) is an accent, never a wash.
- **Generic Amazon-style e-commerce**: dense product grids, aggressive discount/sale badges, cluttered comparison tables, anything that reads mass-market instead of considered.

## Design Principles

1. **Atmosphere before commerce** — every page should feel like a place before it feels like a store. Provenance and ritual lead; the transaction follows.
2. **Colors whisper, not shout** — ivory (#F3EFE7), near-black (#111111), and a single gold accent (#B0885E) at low usage. No competing accent colors.
3. **Minimal text, maximum meaning** — short, weighted copy over dense explanation. Typography and negative space carry hierarchy.
4. **One locale, one language, always** — English (primary) and Arabic (secondary) are never concatenated in the same field; each locale gets clean native content, RTL-correct for Arabic.
5. **Heritage-respectful restraint** — Islamic/Arabian cultural and religious references (Kaaba, Quran, prayer items, mosque imagery) are handled with genuine respect and accuracy, never as generic "exotic" decoration.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AA. Body text ≥4.5:1 contrast against background (watch the ivory/gold combinations specifically — gold-on-ivory text fails AA easily). Large text/headings ≥3:1. All product images require descriptive alt text (EN, with AR as a fast-follow). Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` on all scroll/hover animation (already implemented in the liquid-glass header). Keyboard navigability required for nav, cart, and PDP variant selection.
